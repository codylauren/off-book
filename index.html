A scene partner who never gets tired of running it again.

Upload your script. Off Book finds the characters, reads everyone else's lines out loud, and waits for yours.

Check the breakdown

Click any text to edit it. Press Enter to split a line where your cursor is, or Backspace at the start of a line to join it to the one above. Change who says a line with the menu on the left. Cut keeps a line in the script but skips it in rehearsal, for when the director makes cuts.
